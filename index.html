Good people are easy to miss

Internship seekers can send dozens of applications and still hear nothing. Their CV may be filtered out, buried in an inbox, or never read by the person who could recognise their potential. Professionals face the other side of the gap: it is hard to find thoughtful, motivated people through a crowded LinkedIn feed or generic job website.

Happy You makes a smaller, more relevant bridge. We learn what each person is looking for, suggest introductions by email, and leave the choice with both sides.
